# Catalog marketplace surface

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Treat extensions as inspectable library holdings, not promotional tiles. The surface refuses the familiar app-store wall of interchangeable rounded cards by making provenance, platform, dependencies, and contents visible in the browsing rhythm.

**OWN-WORLD:** A digital accession ledger: ink navy and warm paper, vermilion index tabs, ruled dividers, precise humanist typography, and monospaced text only for identifiers and counts. Controls are compact and rectangular with one restrained corner radius. Linked accession slips make dependencies recognizable even when all copy is removed.

**STORY:** A visitor arrives with a job to solve, searches the combined collection, narrows it by platform and extension type, sees official holdings before community submissions, opens a record, verifies what it contains and where it came from, then follows the source. OpenMausBot appears as a supported peer collection with an honest empty state until entries are contributed.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact masthead sits above a broad paper index. The left two-thirds contain the search statement, live query field, and a dense first run of official records; the right third is an accession panel summarizing the two platforms and four supported record types. The primary action is the search field itself. Selecting a result advances an animated vermilion register mark through its metadata before navigation.

**FORM:** Library accession ledger, candidate 6 of 7 grounded directions. The direction keeps the variable-type challenger's responsive hierarchy, the timetable challenger's column discipline, and the Metro challenger's immediate wayfinding without borrowing their visual clothing.
